## How will it prove* dark matter?
*We can't _prove_ it exists because it needs to be directly observed, but we can find overwhelming evidence for it.

The [Doppler shift](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doppler_effect) will be observed to see how fast hydrogen is moving towards and away from the earth with the formula Δf = f - f₀ = ±f₀(v/c). The simplified equation Δf/f₀ = v/c works just as well, but it's used only to look for an absolute change. Me personally, I like to have every bit of information I can, so I'll use the former formula! Former formula formulated for formality. Fuhrfuhrfuhrfuhroooze! Say _that_ five times fast.

f standing for observed frequency, delta (Δ) f is the doppler shift and f₀ is the frequency the object would emit were it not moving, or rest frequency. v is [radial velocity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radial_velocity) and c is [the speed of light in a vacuum](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speed_of_light)! (you should know this, hopefully...) 

Building onto what was mentioned earlier, 1420 MHz will be used as f₀, and a different frequency observed will be f. A positive doppler shift (also known as blue-shift) means the object is moving towards us, and a negative shift (red-shift) means away. This is relevant because standard Newtonian physics can't really explain how stars on the outer bounds of the Milky Way (or any spiral galaxy, for that matter) move at roughly the same speed as stars anywhere else in the galaxy. Without dark matter, they should just drift away.
