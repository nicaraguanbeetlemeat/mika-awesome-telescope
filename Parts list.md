### A mount we may need to build
And this is where the engineering team comes in. And our sponsor! We could always buy an equatorial mount, but as it stands, those are really, **really** expensive (coming from a former astrophotographer). Just not worth the budget right now. If my school is kind enough though I bet they could give us something, wink wink

A simple [alt-az](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Altitude-azimuth) mount (left, right, up, down) is all we really need for a first telescope; drift scanning (pointing the telescope at a fixed spot and letting Earth's rotation do the moving for you) could be cool anyway.
